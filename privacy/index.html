Shadoo Privacy Policy

  • Application: Shadoo
  • Package name: com.anahronic.shadoo
  • Developer / entity: Anahronic Arts
  • Privacy contact: rubilov@bk.ru
  • Effective date: 8 September 2026

1. Summary

Shadoo runs entirely on your device. It has no server of its own, no user accounts, and no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting components. The developer does not receive, collect, store or have access to your messages, your code words, or any other personal data.

This policy deliberately separates two different things: data that the app processes locally on your device, and data that is collected by the developer. The app does process data locally — that is what an encryption app does. None of it is collected by, or transmitted to, the developer or any third party by the app.

2. What Shadoo does

Two people agree in advance, offline, on one or more secret code words and give each one a number. To send a message you choose the number, enter the code word and your text; the app encrypts it on your device and shows you a single block of encrypted text. You deliver that block yourself, by any means you choose. The recipient pastes it back into Shadoo and enters the same code word to recover the original text.

3. Data processed locally on your device

The following is handled on the device only. It is not transmitted to the developer, and the app has no mechanism to transmit it (see section 5).

  • Code words you type. Used to derive an encryption key. They are not stored in readable form and are not written to the message history.
  • Message text you type. Encrypted on the device. The readable text exists only in memory while the app is unlocked.
  • Encrypted message history. Stored on the device in encrypted form only — see sections 7 and 12.
  • App settings. Interface language, theme, auto-lock on/off and timeout, Strict Offline Mode, Secure Input Mode, and the wipe-on-background option. Stored locally in the app's own storage. These are preferences, not personal data.
  • Clipboard content — only at the moment you tap the paste button, and only to look for an encrypted block. See section 9.
  • An image file you select — only when you use the "Load QR from file" function, and only to read a QR code out of that image. See section 10.

4. Data collected by the developer

None. Shadoo has no user accounts, no registration, no sign-in, no profiles and no identifiers assigned by the developer. It does not collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage statistics. It contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

5. No server, and no network permission

There is no Shadoo server. The Android application does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission, so it cannot send or receive data over the network at all. This can be verified independently by inspecting the permissions of the installed app.

6. Permissions

  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — the only permission the app declares. It is used solely to tell you, in Strict Offline Mode, whether the device currently reports an active network connection. It does not allow the app to transfer any data.
  • The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or storage permissions.

7. Local encryption and history

Messages are encrypted on the device with AES-256-GCM; the key is derived from your code word with Argon2id (or PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 where Argon2id is unavailable). These are published, standard algorithms. Message history is written to the device's local app storage in encrypted form only; readable text is never written there.

If a code word is lost, the corresponding message cannot be recovered — not by you and not by the developer. There is no backup key and no recovery mechanism.

8. Sharing an encrypted message

When you tap Share, Shadoo passes only the encrypted block of text to the Android system share sheet, and only because you initiated that action. It never shares your code word or the readable text of a message.

Once you hand the encrypted text to another application through the share sheet, further handling of it is performed by that third-party application you selected, and is governed by that application's own privacy policy and terms — not by this policy. The same applies to anything you paste, send or upload manually.

9. Clipboard

  • Copy places only the encrypted block on the clipboard. Code words and readable message text are never copied to the clipboard automatically.
  • Paste from clipboard reads the system clipboard only at the moment you tap that button, only to look for an encrypted block, and does not retain the clipboard contents afterwards. The app does not monitor or read the clipboard in the background.
  • Note that the clipboard is a shared area of the operating system. Content you place on it may be visible to the operating system and to other applications.

10. QR codes

  • Display: an encrypted block can be shown on screen as a QR code. The QR code contains only the encrypted block.
  • Save: when you tap Save QR, the app writes that QR code as a PNG image into your device's picture storage (a "Shadoo" folder in Pictures). The image contains only the encrypted block. It is an ordinary file on your device: it remains there until you delete it, and it is subject to any backup or synchronisation you have configured for your gallery.
  • Read from a file: "Load QR from file" reads a QR code from an image you pick yourself. The app does not use the camera and does not request camera permission.

11. Strict Offline Mode and Secure Input Mode

  • Strict Offline Mode shows a checklist before decryption and uses the network-state permission described in section 6 to tell you whether a connection is currently reported. It is a reminder; it does not transmit anything.
  • Secure Input Mode lets you type the code word and the message on a keyboard built into the app instead of the system keyboard, so that a third-party keyboard app is not involved in the typing. Nothing typed this way leaves the device.

12. Retention and deletion of local data

  • Message history is one-time by design. It is erased irrecoverably when the app is closed and again at every subsequent launch.
  • Wipe when the app goes to the background is enabled by default: history is erased when you minimise the app or switch to another app. This can be turned off in Settings.
  • Manual erase: Settings contains a function that irrecoverably erases the whole local history immediately.
  • Manual export: if you use the export function, the app produces a file containing your history in encrypted form only. That file is then yours to keep or delete; the developer never receives it.
  • Settings persist locally until you change them or uninstall the app.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local data from the device. Files you chose to save elsewhere (for example a saved QR image or an exported history file) are not removed by uninstalling and must be deleted by you.

13. Account deletion

Shadoo has no accounts and no server-side storage, so there is no account to delete and no remote data deletion request to make. Deletion is entirely under your control on the device, as described in section 12.

14. Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties, because it does not transmit data at all. Distribution through Google Play is subject to Google's own terms and privacy policy, which apply to the store and to your device, independently of this application.

15. Children

Shadoo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect data.

16. Limitations — stated honestly

  • The strength of the protection depends on the code words you choose. Short or common words are easier to guess than long, unusual phrases.
  • No application can protect data on a device that is already compromised. If the device is infected with spyware or a keylogger, or is under someone else's control, that is outside what this or any other app can remedy.
  • The operating system, the device manufacturer, and other installed applications operate outside this app and are governed by their own policies. This policy describes what Shadoo does, not what the rest of your device does.
  • Anything you deliberately send out of the app — through the share sheet, the clipboard, a saved image or an exported file — leaves the app's control at that point.

17. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app introduces functionality that affects data handling — for example network access, additional permissions, or camera-based QR scanning — this policy will be updated at this same address before that version is published, and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be revised accordingly.
