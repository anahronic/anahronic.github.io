Offline message encryption

Shadoo encrypts a message on your own device using a code word that you and the person you are writing to agree on in advance. The app turns your text into a single block of encrypted text, which you then pass on yourself — through whatever channel you choose. The recipient pastes it back into Shadoo and enters the same code word to read it.

How it works

  • You and the other person agree on code words beforehand, in person.
  • Encryption and decryption happen locally, on the device.
  • The result is a block of encrypted text you can copy, share, or show as a QR code.
  • There is no account and no server: the app does not deliver messages for you.
